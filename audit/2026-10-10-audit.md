# NixWoods site audit, 10 October 2026

Scope: nixwoods.com homepage and the cube lamp product page at mobile width (360px), plus the wall-lights collection page. Method: Impeccable's deterministic detector on the saved HTML, a manual critique of full-page mobile screenshots against `brand/nixwoods/DESIGN.md`, and a read of the page source for fonts and app scripts. Business figures are kept out of this file on purpose; they live in the plan in Drive.

Baseline screenshots: `qa/baseline/2026-10-10/`.

## 1. Detector results (Impeccable, 59 deterministic rules)

72 findings across the two pages.

| Rule | Count | Where | What it means for NixWoods |
|---|---|---|---|
| cramped-padding | 54 | product cards, submenus, FAQ tabs, cart notification | Text sits flush against card and container edges. Cards need 16px inset per DESIGN.md. |
| wide-tracking | 8 | body text with 0.06 to 0.10em letter-spacing | Tracking belongs on the label style only. Body must be 0. |
| undersized-ui-text | 5 | "Extra Rs. 99 off on prepaid orders" at 10px, "Loading.." at 10px | Below the 11px floor. The cart offer line is functional text and must be 13px or more. |
| low-contrast | 2 | white on the WhatsApp green (2.0:1), grey #968d8d on #f4f4f4 (2.9:1) | Both fail 4.5:1. The WhatsApp button needs ink text or a darker ground; the grey helper text needs ink-muted. |
| skipped-heading | 1 | H1 then H3 on the homepage | Product card titles are H3 with no H2 section heading. |
| overused-font | 1 | primary font resolves to Arial on the product page | The theme's font stack falls through to Arial where the custom fonts are not applied. |
| marquee | 1 | the "UP TO 40% OFF" scrolling band | Banned by DESIGN.md motion rules. |

## 2. Manual critique, homepage

Ordered top to bottom as a phone shows it.

1. **Announcement bar**: one offer, fine. But the cart shows a different figure for the same offer. One number.
2. **Header**: NX monogram centred, clean. Keep.
3. **Hero**: a slideshow image with "TABLE LAMPS" baked in as all-caps display text, a tagline baked in at unreadable size, five trust icons baked in at roughly 8px. No live headline, no button, no link target except the image. Against DESIGN.md hero rules on every point.
4. **Headline block** ("Handcrafted Wooden Lights & Lamps") sits below the hero as plain centred text with the WhatsApp chat bubble covering its second line on a 360px screen.
5. **Category circles**: seven round tiles. Serviceable, but "Lamps" duplicates "Table Lamps" and the circles crop the product photos badly.
6. **Promo band and marquee**: "UP TO 40% OFF" repeated, then a scrolling list of category names. Banned.
7. **Best sellers grid**: two columns, no inset padding, percentage-off badges on nine of ten cards, struck-through prices, no star ratings, titles of four to five lines because they are SEO strings. Card images are good.
8. **Collection bands** ("Linear Pendant", "Radiant Lamps"): the same intro paragraph appears three times. One Explore button links to the wrong collection.
9. **Story block**: brand name written as two words.
10. **Trust icons row**: generic icon tiles with "Where Quality Meets Trust" copy. Replace with verifiable facts.
11. **Footer**: brand name as two words again. Links fine.

Fonts loaded: Assistant, Jost, Josefin Sans, Domine. None are in the system.
Grounds: cold white. Buttons: pure black. Neither is in the system.

## 3. Manual critique, cube lamp product page

1. **Gallery**: good hero photo, thumbnails work, but thumbnail two is a marketing card ("Ocean Glass Trio Lamp") rather than a product photo.
2. **Social-proof block** above the title: left as is by decision on 10 October 2026. Not assessed further here.
3. **Title**: five lines of SEO string. The naming system would make it two words plus the wood.
4. **Finish chooser**: three pills, clear. The selected state is black fill; DESIGN.md says amber.
5. **Payment icons row**: a Visa, Google Pay, Apple Pay, PayPal strip. PayPal is not offered at checkout. Remove what is not real.
6. **Add to cart** is a grey bar with a struck-through price inside it; **Buy It Now** is the black primary. Two primaries fight. One amber primary, one secondary.
7. **Review carousel**: shows a review about a pendant light on the cube lamp page. Scope to the product.
8. **Description**: the long-form content is strong and in the right order: hook, finish explainer, moods, specs, where it works, FAQ, buy with confidence, WhatsApp. Keep it. Apply the type system.
9. **WhatsApp button**: white on green fails contrast.
10. **No sticky buy bar** on mobile once the button scrolls away.

## 4. Collection page, wall lights

Strong SEO copy and a six-question FAQ below the grid. The grid itself has the same card problems as the homepage: no ratings, badges, strike-throughs, long titles. Filters are availability and price only; size and up-down versus down-only would help more. No hook, trust strip or review quote at the top, which matters because ads land here.
