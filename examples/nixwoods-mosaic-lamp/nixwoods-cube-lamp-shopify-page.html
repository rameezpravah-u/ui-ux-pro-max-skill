How bright is it?

Soft. It colours a corner; it won't light a desk. If you want the room to feel like a good bar at 10 pm, this is it. Tap "On, at night" above to see it as the only light in a room.

Candy, Ocean or Bubble?

Same lamp, same three colours, different glass. Candy is the sharp grid and photographs strongest. Ocean is a soft ripple, most people's pick in person. Bubble is the most decorative, and this batch has 12.

Size and what's in the box

8 × 8 × 3 in glass block on a 9 × 4.5 in base, bigger than a phone and smaller than a laptop, about 2.6 kg. In the box: the block, the sheesham base with the LED inside and four rubber feet, a 1.5 m cord with plug and inline switch, a care card.

How it's made

One piece of sheesham, Indian rosewood, sanded and oiled by hand in our Khatauli workshop. A real glass block, three faces painted by hand, so up close you'll see brushstrokes. Warm LED built in, 2700 to 3000K, rated 25,000 hours. Three years on the wood, one on the electricals.

Why not the ₹899 plastic cube?

That one is moulded plastic with a printed grid and a remote that loses pairing. This is real glass and solid wood; the colour changes by turning, with no electronics to fail. In five years the wood has darkened and it's still on the table.
