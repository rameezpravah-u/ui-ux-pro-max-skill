Straight answers

What people ask, and what we'd rather you knew first.

From reviews

How bright is it, really?

Soft. It colours a corner; it won't light a desk for work. If you want to read by it, pair it with an overhead. If you want the room to feel like a good bar at 10 pm, this is it.

From reviews

Does the block sit firm?

It rests in a routed channel so you can lift and turn it. It should sit flat and still. If yours rocks, send a photo and we sort it under warranty.

From reviews

Is the paint perfect?

No. The faces are brushed by hand, so up close you'll see strokes. Lit, it's even. Unlit, it looks hand-made because it is.

From WhatsApp

Candy, Ocean or Bubble?

Same lamp, same three colours, different glass. Candy is the sharp grid and photographs strongest. Ocean is a soft ripple, most people's pick in person. Bubble is the most decorative, and this batch has 12.

From WhatsApp

Can I return it if I don't like it?

No. Each base is cut and finished for your order, so we don't take change-of-mind returns or cancellations. That's why we'll show you the lamp on a video call first, send a photo of yours before dispatch, and replace it free if it arrives damaged or wrong. Ships in about 3 working days, delivered in 5 to 7.

From WhatsApp

Is it an actual Rubik's Cube? Cordless?

Neither. It's an original design that borrows the grid; you can't twist the rows. It plugs into the wall with a 1.5 m cord. Tell us if cordless matters and we'll count the asks.
