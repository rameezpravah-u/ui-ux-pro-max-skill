Asked in reviews

How bright is it, really?

Soft. It's a mood light that colours a corner, not a lamp you read the paper by. If you were hoping for a desk light, this isn't it. If you want the room to feel like a good bar at 10 pm, it is.

Asked in reviews

Does the block sit firm?

The block rests in a routed channel on the base so you can lift and turn it. It should sit flat and still. If yours rocks, message us a photo and we sort it, that's what the warranty is for.

Asked in reviews

Is the paint perfect?

No, and we'd rather say so. The faces are brushed by hand, so up close you'll see strokes and slight variation. Lit, it's even. Unlit, it looks hand-made because it is.

Questions

Before you decide.

Candy, Ocean or Bubble. Which one?

Same lamp, same three colours, different glass texture. Candy is the faceted grid, the sharpest and most graphic. Ocean is a smooth ripple, softer and moodier when lit. Bubble mixes waves and bubbles, the most decorative of the three. Candy photographs strongest; Ocean is what most people pick after seeing both in person.

How do the three colours work?

Three of the block's faces are hand-painted red, green and amber. Turn the block so a different face sits toward you, and the warm LED underneath glows through it in that colour. Fully manual. No remote, no app, nothing to charge or break.

Is this an actual Rubik's Cube?

No. It's an original NixWoods design that borrows the grid. It's a lamp, not a puzzle, and you can't twist the rows.

Can I replace the LED?

It's built in and rated for 25,000 hours, about ten years at seven hours a night. If it ever fails inside the first year we replace the lamp; after that we repair it.

How do I look after the wood?

Wipe with a dry cloth. Rub a little food-safe oil into the base every six months or so and the sheesham darkens beautifully. Keep the glass out of the dishwasher.

Cash on delivery?

Yes, across India, with a small handling fee at checkout. Prepaid orders ship free.
