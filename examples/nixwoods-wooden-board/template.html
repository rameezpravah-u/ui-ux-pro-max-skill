Care

Three habits. A lifetime of use.

Solid wood asks very little. Do these and the board will look better at ten years than it did on day one.

After every use

Hand wash, wipe dry.

Warm water, a little soap, a cloth. Never soak it and never the dishwasher. Wood that's left wet on one side will cup.

While it dries

Stand it on its edge.

Air on both faces means it dries evenly and stays flat. The hanging hole is there for exactly this.

Once a month

Oil it. Watch the grain wake up.

A spoonful of food-grade mineral or coconut oil, rubbed in and left overnight. The colour deepens and the surface seals itself again.

Questions

Everything people ask before they buy.

Will my board look exactly like the photos?

No, and that's the point. The shape, size, wood and finish will match. The grain pattern will be your board's own. If you'd like to see the actual board before it ships, message us on WhatsApp and we'll send a photo of the underside with your number.

Can I chop on a serving board, or serve on a chopping board?

Yes to both. Every board is the same solid hardwood at 18 to 22 mm. The names describe the shape's best use, not a limit. The small teak is simply the handiest for daily chopping; the sheesham is the most striking on a table.

Will it dull my knives?

No. Teak and sheesham are dense but not hard enough to damage a steel edge, unlike glass, stone or bamboo laminate. Knives glide across the grain.

How do I look after it?

Hand wash with warm water, wipe, and stand it on its edge to dry. Rub in a spoonful of food-grade mineral or coconut oil once a month. Never soak it and never put it in a dishwasher.

How long until it arrives?

Because each board is made after you order, allow 3 to 5 working days for delivery anywhere in India. You'll get a tracking link by SMS and email once it leaves the workshop.

Where are they made?

In our own workshop in Khatauli, Uttar Pradesh, by the same team that makes NixWoods lighting and furniture.

What if it arrives damaged?

Send us a photo within 7 days and we replace it. Because each board is one of one we can't offer change-of-mind returns, which is why we show you everything on this page first.
