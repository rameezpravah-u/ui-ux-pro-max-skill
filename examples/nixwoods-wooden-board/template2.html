Questions

Before you decide.

Why is it more than a board from the market?

A market board is glued strips of whatever was left over, and it will split in a year or two. This is one slab of teak or sheesham, 18 to 22 mm thick, that will outlive the kitchen. Over ten years the sheesham costs under a rupee a day.

What does "reserve" actually do?

It holds the next registry number for you for 72 hours with no payment. If you buy in that window, that number is yours. If not, it goes back in the book and we let you know before it does.

Will my board look exactly like the photos?

No, and that's the point. Shape, size, wood and finish will match. The grain will be your board's own. You'll get a photo of your actual board before it ships.

Can I chop on the serving boards?

Yes. They're all the same solid hardwood. The names describe the shape's best use, not a limit.

How do I pay monthly?

Choose EMI at checkout. Three months, no interest, on cards and UPI-linked EMI. The board is cut as soon as the first payment clears.

What if it arrives damaged?

Send a photo within 7 days and we replace it. No change-of-mind returns, because nobody else can have your number.
