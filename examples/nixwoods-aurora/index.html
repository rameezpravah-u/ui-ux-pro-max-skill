Questions people ask

Which length should I choose?

36", 40" or 48", all 1.75" wide and 1.25" deep. Match the length to the wall: 36" for a bedside or a compact wall, 48" for a corridor or a large feature wall. The scale drawing above shows each against a queen headboard.

Which finish should I pick?

Dark brown is a deep stain that reads as a dark line. Natural teak is unstained and shows the open grain. Both have the same warm 3000K LED.

Can I choose which way the light shines?

Yes. Every length comes with the light facing down only. The 36" and 48" also come facing up and down, which washes the whole wall.

Is it really solid wood?

Yes. Solid teak with the grain running the full length. Not MDF, not veneer, so no two pieces are exactly alike.

Do I need an electrician?

We advise one unless you know basic electrical connections. Two screws hold it to the wall and one wire connects to your existing point. Switch the power off at the mains first. The LED is built in, so there are no bulbs to buy.

Is it for indoor use only?

Yes. Bedrooms, hallways, living rooms and similar dry rooms. Not for bathrooms, balconies or outdoors.

How long is the warranty?

Three years on the solid teak, joinery and finish, and one year on the LED and electricals, both from the day it is delivered. Natural movement in the grain is not a defect; it is proof the wood is real.

How long does delivery take?

Dispatched in about 3 working days, then roughly 5 to 7 working days to reach you. You get the courier and tracking details by email once it ships. Shipping is free on prepaid orders.
